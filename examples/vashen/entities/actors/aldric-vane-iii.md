Third of his name, and the reason his grandson is the fourth. The chapterhouse has him
holding the Vale for twenty-seven years and gives no date for either end of it — the
records of that century are a tax roll and a list of grievances, and neither says when a
duke died.

Two vague dates meeting at the same year is what a succession looks like when nobody was
writing it down: the handover to Maren is *unsettled* rather than contested, and the
difference matters. Compare it with the one below him, which the chronicle pins to the day.
